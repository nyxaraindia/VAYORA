# VAYORA

**Passenger-first public transportation information for Kerala.**

VAYORA is a proposed platform to help people discover participating buses, routes, stops, service times, and changing passenger occupancy. The project is starting with a focused pilot: prove that a small, reliable end-to-end service can help passengers before expanding to more corridors and districts.

> **Project status:** Early proposal / pilot planning. Features described below are goals, not a claim that a public service is already available.

## Why VAYORA?

Planning a bus journey can mean piecing together information from different places—or relying on information that is difficult to find. VAYORA aims to make useful, locally relevant public-transport information easier to discover, while keeping the passenger experience clear and dependable.

## What the pilot aims to explore

- **Participating bus services:** Find the services included in the pilot.
- **Routes and stops:** Understand where a participating service travels and where passengers can board or get off.
- **Service times:** Find the available schedule information for a service.
- **Passenger occupancy:** See changing occupancy information where it can be collected and communicated reliably.
- **A dependable end-to-end experience:** Test how transport information is gathered, maintained, and presented—not just how it looks in an app.

Availability and freshness of information will depend on participating services and the data available to the pilot. VAYORA should clearly communicate uncertainty rather than imply that unverified information is live or guaranteed.

## Pilot principles

1. **Start small:** Focus on a defined corridor or set of participating services.
2. **Be useful to passengers:** Prioritize clear, easy-to-understand journey information.
3. **Earn trust:** Make the source and freshness of information understandable, especially for changing occupancy data.
4. **Build for Kerala:** Account for local routes, travel practices, and the needs of passengers in the pilot area.
5. **Expand based on evidence:** Use pilot results and participant feedback to guide future coverage.

## Who it is for

- **Passengers** looking for practical information about participating bus services.
- **Operators and service partners** who may help provide or maintain transport information.
- **Contributors and local stakeholders** interested in improving access to public-transport information in Kerala.

## Project status and next steps

VAYORA is at an early stage. The initial focus is to define the pilot area, identify participating services and information sources, and validate a reliable way to keep core service information useful and up to date. Broader coverage should follow only when the pilot demonstrates that the service can be maintained reliably.

This repository is the home for the project’s planning and implementation as they take shape. More details—such as the pilot area, product design, data sources, and technical setup—will be added as they are confirmed.
